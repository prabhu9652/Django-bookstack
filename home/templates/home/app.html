Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PWA?

A Progressive Web App (PWA) is a website that can be installed on your device and works like a native app, with offline support and fast loading.

Is it free?

Yes! The app is completely free. No app store, no downloads, no charges.

Does it take storage space?

Very minimal. PWAs are lightweight compared to native apps, typically using only a few MB.

Can I uninstall it?

Yes, just like any other app. Right-click the icon and select uninstall/remove.
