Understanding Career Documents

What You Need to Know

What is a Resume?

A resume is a concise document (1-2 pages) that summarizes your professional experience, skills, education, and achievements. It's your first impression with employers and is scanned by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before reaching human recruiters.

Tailored to each job Quantified achievements ATS-friendly format

What is a Cover Letter?

A cover letter is a personalized letter that accompanies your resume. It explains why you're interested in the role, highlights relevant experience, and shows your personality. It's your chance to tell your story beyond bullet points.

Company-specific Shows enthusiasm Complements resume
